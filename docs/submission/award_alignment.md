# Award and judging alignment — evidence plan

AgroVision's strongest intended case is a local rotation decision supported by traceable NASA evidence. This is an evidence checklist, not a claim of award eligibility or a prediction of winning. The 2026 [Judging & Awards Guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/judging-awards-guide/) could not be retrieved. Award names below are from the official [2025 award page](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2025/awards/) and remain provisional for 2026. No numeric weights are assumed.

## General judging criteria

The repository's [award analysis](../08_premios.md) records Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance and Presentation from prior guidance and 2026 local-event pages. Exact 2026 definitions/weights remain unverified.

| Criterion | Show in AgroVision | Current evidence and remaining work |
|---|---|---|
| Impact | A plausible decision benefit for San Juan farmers and technicians | Four-pillar rotation framing is documented. User testing, farm outcomes, water savings and income effects are unmeasured. |
| Creativity | Image-derived bare-field timing and irrigation/rainfed signals connected to crop sequences | Core analysis design documented; final classifier evidence and validation pending. Market data complement the NASA story. |
| Validity | Traceable source → transformation → metric → limitation | Market pipeline, checksum, original units, missing-data handling and tests delivered. NASA provenance and classification checks must be completed in the integrated build. |
| Relevance | Current rotation plus at least two illustrative alternatives using actual challenge NASA data | Product direction matches Field Shift. Baseline local confirmation, scenario/rule evidence and final UI verification remain pending. |
| Presentation | One clear decision, readable English, sources visible, accessible links | Summary, 29-second script and seven-slide outline delivered as drafts. Recording/export, final URLs and actual 2026 limits remain pending. |

## Award opportunities and proof needed

| Provisional category | AgroVision angle | Concrete proof required / limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Best Use of Data | Make NASA and official local evidence understandable for a rotation comparison | Show an actual NASA dataset driving a scenario metric, with period, unit, scale and uncertainty. A planned source list or market-only demo is insufficient. |
| Best Use of Science | Explain assumptions and separate observations, reanalysis, agronomic rules and model estimates | Present quality masks, source methods, classification validation and the published rule behind each comparison. No scientific validation claimed without the evidence. |
| Best Use of Technology | Reproducible image analysis and a cached demonstration path | Demonstrate classifier behavior and the local data path. The market export already reproduces offline; the full app requires verification. A generative chat interface is not the core contribution. |
| Local Impact | Focus on the Valle de San Juan and actual local review needs | Cite relevant Dominican sources, confirm the baseline with a technician and show how missing information affects a decision. No invented interviews or measured impact. |
| Galactic Impact | Potential for more resilient agricultural decisions | Explain the intended benefit and a realistic validation route. Replication elsewhere is future work, not demonstrated reach. |
| Best Mission Concept | A bounded, plausible MVP | Keep one pilot, two or three alternatives, explicit constraints and feasible next steps. Sensors remain outside the demo. |
| Most Inspirational | Respectful story about a consequential agricultural choice | Use an illustrative decision without attributing it to a real farmer or inventing testimony. |
| Best Use of Storytelling | Follow evidence into a decision and end with human judgment | Produce the short English demo, show NASA evidence and disclose gaps. Confirm duration in the active portal. |
| Global Connection | Reusable method with local knowledge | Document reproducible ingestion and the local adaptation required. Do not imply partnerships, users or a global network that are not verified. |
| Art & Technology | Clear maps, timelines and comparison graphics | Legends, readable attribution and accessible states in the actual build. AI-created media, if later added, require labeling and appropriate rights. |

## Review priorities before recording

1. Establish the actual NASA-data path in the frozen app; verify at least one used dataset is in Field Shift's Resources list.
2. Confirm the current rotation and label alternatives as illustrative. Explain all four pillars; pest rules are not outbreak forecasts and wholesale prices are not farm income.
3. Show image analysis with honest validation status. Do not convert a proposed algorithm into an accuracy claim.
4. Keep missing seasonal indices visible. The conservative export provides pigeon-pea observations but no supported pigeon-pea seasonal index.
5. Complete Frainer review, licenses, AI disclosure and anonymous link checks; submit through the team's portal and retain confirmation.
