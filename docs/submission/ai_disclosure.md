## How outputs are checked and attributed

- Price records are traceable to the original MA CSV and checksum. Missing/zero prices, unknown units and ambiguous periods are not invented or silently imputed. Tests cover invalid data, duplicate conflicts, explicit unit conversions and incomplete seasonal coverage.
- New substantial code/Markdown files have an AI attribution header. The JSON export has `aiAssistance` metadata; source CSV bytes remain unchanged. Non-commentable source metadata and data provenance are attributed in `AI_USAGE.md` and the market README.
- This task generated no synthetic image, audio or finished video. If the team later adds such media, disclose the actual tool and contribution and follow the [GO Team's AI labeling clarification](https://github.com/nasa/spaceapps/discussions/1849). AI may not generate or alter NASA logos or other branding elements.
- Runtime explanations, if enabled, should receive only non-sensitive computed metrics, display an AI-generated label and never supply missing measurements. No producer identity, exact private geometry, credentials or personal data should be included in prompts.
